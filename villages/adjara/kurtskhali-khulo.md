## History
- The Soviet Georgian SSR administrative directory for 1 May 1960 lists Kurtskhali (Курцхали) in Khulo District. ([source](https://djvu.online/file/KpnepFgpi27bv))
- The 2002 census reports ethnic Georgians as 99% of the village population. ([source](https://geostat.ge/media/44560/II-tomi-.pdf#page=41))
- A Georgian Technical University hydrometeorological survey records destructive avalanches at Kurtskhali in 1959 and 1971. ([source](https://openlibrary.ge/bitstream/123456789/10195/1/IHM-14-17.pdf))
- In December 2016, Batumelebi reported nearly three metres of snow in the village, a road closure lasting several days and a power cut. ([source](https://batumelebi.netgazeti.ge/adjara/62933/))
- Visitajara describes families returning to their homes in summer and very few people staying through winter. The tourism profile is undated. ([source](https://www.toyo.ge/en/kurtskhali-alpine-ajara))
- Founding date, pre-Soviet records, family names, confirmed causes of permanent depopulation, and the last permanent household or departure year — nothing found.
