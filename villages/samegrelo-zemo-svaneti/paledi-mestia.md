## History
- Paledi appears in Johann Blaramberg’s early-19th-century description of the Pari district, in a list of settlements; the passage documents its existence by that time but gives no founding date or account of who lived there. A 2017 travel report describes it as completely abandoned and notes visible ruins; it does not identify when it emptied or its last residents. ([source](https://drevlit.ru/docs/kavkaz/XIX/1820-1840/Blaramberg/text24.php))
- A Russian-language report on a 2017 ski tour describes Paledi as entirely abandoned and visible from a distance by its large ruins. It supplies no reliable date or cause of abandonment and names no last inhabitants. ([source](https://risk.ru/blog/211101))
