## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Чиго and lists all 182 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Elanidze, Markhvaidze, Bakuridze, Meshvlidze. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- The village survey traces Bakuridze and Akimidze to the Ochoidze family and records the Elanidze, Meshvelidze, Markhvaidze and Bekuridze lines. ([source](https://www.dzeglebi.ge/mxareebi_qalaqebi/tusheti/tusheti_chigo.html))
- The 2014 protected-areas management plan dates forced Soviet resettlement from Tusheti to the lowlands to the late 1950s. This describes the regional policy. ([source](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/2190286))

## Population Over Time (Other Sources)
- 1855 — 111 residents. R. Eristov’s village table, published in 1855, lists Чиго with 23 houses, 54 men and 57 women. ([source](https://www.vostlit.info/Texts/Dokumenty/Kavkaz/XIX/1840-1860/Eristov_R/text1.htm))
- 1917 — 182 residents. Tiflis Governorate village table, transcribed as Чиго. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- 1923 — 127 residents. Ethnic composition of rural Georgia by settlement, transcribed as chigho (Chigho). ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1923.htm))
- 1930–31 — 147 residents. Sergi Makalatia’s 1933 ethnographic survey, printed pp. 86–89, lists 32 households. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- 2014 — 2 residents. Geostat census as transcribed in the settlement table. Geostat’s current downloadable workbook masks the small count. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-census2014.htm))
- 2024 — 1 resident. Geostat census on 14 November 2024, workbook row 1512. ([source](https://geostat.ge/media/81091/1.Population-of-Georgia-by-administrative-territorial-units-and-sex.xlsx))

## Crafts And Traditions
- Makalatia’s 1930–31 survey records livestock keeping here, with 1,514 sheep and goats and 208 cattle. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- The village survey records the feast at the Chighostavi shrine on the Lasharoba cycle, with brewing, a youth horse race and a meal of bread, meat, cheese and small beer. ([source](https://www.dzeglebi.ge/mxareebi_qalaqebi/tusheti/tusheti_chigo.html))
- Makalatia’s account of the Lashari observance in Chigho describes households contributing barley for festival beer and providing bread and mutton for offerings. ([source](https://mdevari.ge/articles/4225/))

## Churches, Towers, Monuments
- The shrine of Lashari is identified as Tusheti’s principal shrine in the ethnographic inventory. Separate designation nothing found. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/a-list-of-all-the-shrines-of-pshavi-khevsureti-tusheti.html))
