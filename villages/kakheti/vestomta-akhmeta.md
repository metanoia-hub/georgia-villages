## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Вестомта and lists all 58 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Kakhilauri, Apshilaidze. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- Mtis Ambebi reported in December 2022 that Vestomta had been without a permanent resident for decades. It interviewed Giorgi Karsamauli, who had worked seasonally at his family’s farm and then emigrated to Ireland in 2022, citing the economics of sheep farming and bureaucracy. ([source](https://mtisambebi.ge/news/item/1580))
- The 2014 protected-areas management plan dates forced Soviet resettlement from Tusheti to the lowlands to the late 1950s. This describes the regional policy. ([source](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/2190286))

## Crafts And Traditions
- Makalatia’s 1930–31 survey records livestock keeping here, with 739 sheep and goats and 62 cattle. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- Shepherd Giorgi Karsamauli produced guda cheese in Vestomta using an aging method he developed. He also converted a house into a guesthouse, bringing in building materials by horse. ([source](https://mtisambebi.ge/news/item/1580))
