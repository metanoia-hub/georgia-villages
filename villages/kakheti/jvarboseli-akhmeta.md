## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Джварбосели and lists all 162 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Kardiladze, Geslaidze. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- Shavkhelishvili cites a 1831 archival document, Georgian Central Archive, fond 254, inventory 1, naming a Shavtsqalashvili family in Jvarboseli. ([source](https://mematiane.ge/product-details.php?id=9581))

## Population Over Time (Other Sources)
- 1855 — 113 residents. R. Eristov’s village table, published in 1855, lists Джвар-Босели with 24 houses, 67 men and 46 women. ([source](https://www.vostlit.info/Texts/Dokumenty/Kavkaz/XIX/1840-1860/Eristov_R/text1.htm))
- 1876 — 25 houses or hearths. Gustav Radde’s firsthand account of his summer visit. ([source](https://www.vostlit.info/Texts/Dokumenty/Kavkaz/XIX/1860-1880/Radde_G_I/text9.htm))
- 1917 — 162 residents. Tiflis Governorate village table, transcribed as Джварбосели. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))

- 1930–31 — 44 residents. Sergi Makalatia’s 1933 ethnographic survey, printed pp. 86–89, lists 9 households. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))

## Crafts And Traditions
- Makalatia’s 1930–31 survey records livestock keeping here, with 625 sheep and goats and 68 cattle. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- Makalatia records Khitanoba at the St George of Khitana shrine on a Monday during the Atnigenoba cycle. ([source](https://mdevari.ge/articles/4225/))

## Churches, Towers, Monuments
- The ethnographic shrine inventory records St George of Khitana, Holy Mary and St Theodore above the village, as well as the fortress of the sacred banner. Individual heritage designation nothing found. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/a-list-of-all-the-shrines-of-pshavi-khevsureti-tusheti.html))
- In 1876 Gustav Radde described a slate community church in a meadow below the houses. Separate heritage designation nothing found. ([source](https://www.vostlit.info/Texts/Dokumenty/Kavkaz/XIX/1860-1880/Radde_G_I/text9.htm))
