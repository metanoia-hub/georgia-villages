## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Дано and lists all 179 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Tatrulaidze, Khelaidze. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- The village survey records roughly forty households in the late 19th century and a gradual Soviet-era depopulation. It names, among others, the Tavberidze, Alkhanadze, Shakhadze, Khelaidze and Omaidze families, and records descendants returning in summer from Kakheti. ([source](https://www.dzeglebi.ge/mxareebi_qalaqebi/tusheti/tusheti_dano.html))
- The 2014 protected-areas management plan dates forced Soviet resettlement from Tusheti to the lowlands to the late 1950s. This describes the regional policy. ([source](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/2190286))
