## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Вестмо and lists all 103 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Ichvaidze, Rainauli. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- Shavkhelishvili cites 1878 parish records naming Ichuaidze, Rainauli, Isashvili and Kakushadze households. His 1967 fieldwork also records Utonaurebi. ([source](https://mematiane.ge/product-details.php?id=9581))

## Population Over Time (Other Sources)
- 1855 — 52 residents. R. Eristov’s village table, published in 1855, lists Вестма with 9 houses, 26 men and 26 women. ([source](https://www.vostlit.info/Texts/Dokumenty/Kavkaz/XIX/1840-1860/Eristov_R/text1.htm))
- 1878 — 7 households. Iliurta church records, as cited by Abram Shavkhelishvili. ([source](https://mematiane.ge/product-details.php?id=9581))
- 1917 — 103 residents. Tiflis Governorate village table, transcribed as Вестмо. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- 1923 — 85 residents. Ethnic composition of rural Georgia by settlement, transcribed as vestmo (Vestmo). ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1923.htm))
- 1930–31 — 71 residents. Sergi Makalatia’s 1933 ethnographic survey, printed pp. 86–89, lists 15 households. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
