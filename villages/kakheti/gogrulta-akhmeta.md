## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Горгулта and lists all 85 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Borshoidze, Khabaidze. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- A hiking account documents the ruins of Gogrulta’s former winter homes, also called Lashkaraulta, down the slope from the summer village. ([source](https://www.caucasus-trekking.com/treks/hike-from-khiso-to-gogrulta))

## Crafts And Traditions
- Makalatia’s 1930–31 survey records livestock keeping here, with 830 sheep and goats and 81 cattle. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- A field report documents hospitality at the guesthouse founded by Sokrat and Lily and later run by their daughter Marina. ([source](https://www.caucasus-trekking.com/treks/hike-from-khiso-to-gogrulta))
