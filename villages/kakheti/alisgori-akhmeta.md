## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Алисгори and lists all 56 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Jaadze. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- Travelling through the valley in 1876, Gustav Radde recorded Alisgori as Алис-гори and described houses and towers on the ridge above its stream. ([source](https://www.vostlit.info/Texts/Dokumenty/Kavkaz/XIX/1860-1880/Radde_G_I/text9.htm))
