## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Чешо and lists all 71 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Abaloidze, Omaidze. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- The village survey records gradual Soviet-era depopulation, including moves to lowland Kakheti and summer returns. It names Utsoidze, Omaidze, Alkhanaidze, Kavtaridze and Abaloidze families. ([source](https://www.dzeglebi.ge/mxareebi_qalaqebi/tusheti/tusheti_chesho.html))
- In February 2019, Mtis Ambebi interviewed Zurab Abaloidze, who had stayed through the winter since October 2018, about 55 years after the previous winter resident, Mose Kaaidze, died. ([source](https://mtisambebi.ge/news/people/item/902))
- The 2014 protected-areas management plan dates forced Soviet resettlement from Tusheti to the lowlands to the late 1950s. This describes the regional policy. ([source](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/2190286))

## Crafts And Traditions
- Makalatia’s 1930–31 survey records livestock keeping here, with 1,965 sheep and goats and 115 cattle. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- Zurab Abaloidze described keeping cattle, milking, sowing and cutting hay. The reporter records him serving kalti and khavitsi. ([source](https://mtisambebi.ge/news/people/item/902))
