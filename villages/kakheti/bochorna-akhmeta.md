## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Бочорма and lists all 135 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Otiuridze, Kakhoidze, Lilauri. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- Chai Khana reports that physician Irakli Khvedaguridze moved to Bochorna in 2012 and was its only resident at the time of its reporting. It describes him providing medical care to neighbouring villages. ([source](https://chaikhana.media/en/stories/757/tusheti-a-split-sense-of-home))
- Mtis Ambebi identifies Irakli Khvedaguridze as the permanent resident recorded in 2014. The report says the village had no official village status and no residents at the time of the 2002 census. ([source](https://mtisambebi.ge/news/business/item/93-bochorna-%E2%80%93-ybvelaze-magali-sopeli-evropashi))
- Shavkhelishvili’s interview with Petre Otiuridze names the Otiuridze, Didauridze or Lilauridze, Khvedaguridze, Ivachidze, Kakhoidze, Choganaidze and Mishidze families. ([source](https://mematiane.ge/product-details.php?id=9581))

## Population Over Time (Other Sources)
- 1855 — 144 residents. R. Eristov’s village table, published in 1855, lists Бочорма with 27 houses, 68 men and 76 women. ([source](https://www.vostlit.info/Texts/Dokumenty/Kavkaz/XIX/1840-1860/Eristov_R/text1.htm))
- 1917 — 135 residents. Tiflis Governorate village table, transcribed as Бочорма. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- 1923 — 90 residents. Ethnic composition of rural Georgia by settlement, transcribed as bochorma (Bochorma). ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1923.htm))
- 1930–31 — 51 residents. Sergi Makalatia’s 1933 ethnographic survey, printed pp. 86–89, lists 15 households. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- 2014 — 1 resident. Geostat census as transcribed in the settlement table. Geostat’s current downloadable workbook masks the small count. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-census2014.htm))
- 2024 — 1 resident. Geostat census on 14 November 2024, workbook row 1478. ([source](https://geostat.ge/media/81091/1.Population-of-Georgia-by-administrative-territorial-units-and-sex.xlsx))
