## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Парсма and lists all 308 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Kaaidze, Sulakauri. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- The village survey names Kaaidze, Torghvaidze and Sulakauri families. The Kaaidze family was the most numerous in that account. ([source](https://www.dzeglebi.ge/mxareebi_qalaqebi/tusheti/tusheti_farsma.html))
- The 2014 protected-areas management plan dates forced Soviet resettlement from Tusheti to the lowlands to the late 1950s. This describes the regional policy. ([source](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/2190286))

## Crafts And Traditions
- Makalatia’s 1930–31 survey records livestock keeping here, with 662 sheep and goats and 103 cattle. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- The village survey records a carved wooden grain chest in the Kaaidze tower and attributes construction of the pointed-roof tower to a Kist father and son from Baulo. ([source](https://www.dzeglebi.ge/mxareebi_qalaqebi/tusheti/tusheti_farsma.html))
- Makalatia records Atnigenoba at the St George shrine on 6 August, new style, with a wrestling custom known as Shete and Shamil. ([source](https://mdevari.ge/articles/4225/))

## Churches, Towers, Monuments
- The historic site of Parsma is dated to the late medieval period and listed as a monument of national significance, register entry 7517. ([source](https://www.heritagesites.ge/uploads/files/6068808f2b2f2.pdf#page=266))
- The Heritage Agency describes around fifty structures. The village survey documents fortified houses and a pointed-roof communal tower. ([source](https://www.heritagesites.ge/ka/news_item/253))
- The ethnographic shrine inventory records Karate, St Theodore, St Nino and St George. Individual heritage designation nothing found. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/a-list-of-all-the-shrines-of-pshavi-khevsureti-tusheti.html))
- Late medieval fortified houses of national significance. The Heritage Agency lists it under register entry 994, dated 3 October 2007. ([source](https://www.heritagesites.ge/uploads/files/6068808f2b2f2.pdf#page=32))
