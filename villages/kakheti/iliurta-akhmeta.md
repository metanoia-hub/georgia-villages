## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Илурта and lists all 128 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Kheloidze, Khetsauridze. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))

## Population Over Time (Other Sources)
- 1855 — 113 residents. R. Eristov’s village table, published in 1855, lists Илиурта with 21 houses, 57 men and 56 women. ([source](https://www.vostlit.info/Texts/Dokumenty/Kavkaz/XIX/1840-1860/Eristov_R/text1.htm))
- 1917 — 128 residents. Tiflis Governorate village table, transcribed as Илурта. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- 1923 — 37 residents. Ethnic composition of rural Georgia by settlement, transcribed as iliurta (Iliurta). ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1923.htm))
- 1930–31 — 13 residents. Sergi Makalatia’s 1933 ethnographic survey, printed pp. 86–89, lists 7 households. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
