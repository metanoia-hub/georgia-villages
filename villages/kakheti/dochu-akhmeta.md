## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Дочо and lists all 190 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Abulidze, Torghvaidze, Tsomikuridze. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- Ekofact described Dochu as a former village in 2012 and reported its surviving houses and fortified towers. ([source](https://ekofact.com/2012/09/06/%E1%83%97%E1%83%A3%E1%83%A8%E1%83%94%E1%83%97%E1%83%98-%E1%83%93%E1%83%9D%E1%83%AD%E1%83%A3/))

## Crafts And Traditions
- Makalatia’s 1930–31 survey records livestock keeping here, with 1,554 sheep and goats and 166 cattle. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- Ekofact records stone construction of the village’s towers and homes. ([source](https://ekofact.com/2012/09/06/%E1%83%97%E1%83%A3%E1%83%A8%E1%83%94%E1%83%97%E1%83%98-%E1%83%93%E1%83%9D%E1%83%AD%E1%83%A3/))

## Churches, Towers, Monuments
- Late medieval fortified house. The Heritage Agency register records it under entry 943, dated 3 October 2007. ([source](https://www.heritagesites.ge/uploads/files/6068808f2b2f2.pdf#page=30))
- The ethnographic shrine inventory records St George the Swarthy, also called Kopala, and the Khakhmati and Karate niches. Individual heritage designation nothing found. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/a-list-of-all-the-shrines-of-pshavi-khevsureti-tusheti.html))
- The village itself entered the Heritage Agency register in 2016 as entry 7311, with fabric dated to the late medieval period and the 19th–20th centuries. ([source](https://www.heritagesites.ge/uploads/files/6068808f2b2f2.pdf#page=256))
- The Protected Landscape administration records a 28 October 2016 designation order for the settlement and identifies six immovable heritage monuments within it. ([source](https://www.tushetipl.ge/article/8))
