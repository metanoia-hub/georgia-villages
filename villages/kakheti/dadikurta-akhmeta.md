## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Дадикурта and lists all 64 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- Makalatia’s 1933 survey records the following local surnames in 1930–31. Khetsoidze, Kokilauri. ([source](https://www.batsav.com/pages/tusheti-population-census-makalatia.html))
- Shavkhelishvili records former resident Saba Babulaidze’s account of seven households, including the Kheloidze, Babulaidze and Vepkhiaidze families. ([source](https://mematiane.ge/product-details.php?id=9581))
