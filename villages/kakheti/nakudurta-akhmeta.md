## History
- The 1917 Tiflis Governorate table records the settlement as Накудурта and lists all 28 residents as Georgian. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1917-east.htm))
- The architectural survey records three Kelekhidze households before abandonment after 1917, with moves to Girevi and the lowland villages of Kakheti. ([source](https://www.dzeglebi.ge/mxareebi_qalaqebi/tusheti/tusheti_nakudurta.html))

## Population Over Time (Other Sources)
