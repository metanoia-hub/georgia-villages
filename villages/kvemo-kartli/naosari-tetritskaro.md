## History
- Naosari is recorded as a settlement by 1917, when it had 87 residents. It had 61 residents in 1923, all recorded as Georgian in the rural settlement table, and 24 in 2002. The 2014 census was the first of the listed modern censuses with zero residents. The sources found do not explain why it emptied or identify the last inhabitants. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1923.htm))
- A 1960 administrative directory lists Naosari in Tetritskaro District. This establishes the Soviet-era administrative name, but not its founding or first historical mention. ([source](https://djvu.online/file/KpnepFgpi27bv))
