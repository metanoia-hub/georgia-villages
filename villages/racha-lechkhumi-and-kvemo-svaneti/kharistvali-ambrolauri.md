## History
- This page appears to refer to Kharistvala, the former townlet beside the Shaori Reservoir in Ambrolauri Municipality. A settlement history says workers and geologists lived there during the 1950s construction of the Shaori–Tkibuli hydroelectric scheme; the place was designated a resort and received townlet status in 1956. After the project ended and resort activity did not develop, it depopulated. A secondary source attributes the absence of permanent residents after 1991 to the earthquake, but the material located does not establish that as the sole cause. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharistvala))
- A Georgian travel article describes the Shaori basin and notes that the settlement of Kharistvala stands on the western shore; the historical natural feature called Kharistvali Lake is a different namesake and should not be confused with the settlement. ([source](https://rentup.ge/ka/attractions/shaori-reservoir/))
