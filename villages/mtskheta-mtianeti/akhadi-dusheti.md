## History
- No founded or first-recorded date was found. Akhadi is the birthplace of poet and anti-Soviet partisan Mikha Khelashvili, born there in 1900. A 2017 interview describes the village as having once had about 70 households (a resident’s recollection), with only four or five houses occupied seasonally in the period before it emptied. ([source](https://mtisambebi.ge/news/people/item/368-%E2%80%9Eaxadshi-dabrunebaz-gvinda-da-darchenaz%2C-magram-arz-gzaa-da-arz-kvali%E2%80%9C))
- The same report says two families had left permanently before the interviewee’s family also moved away in 2007. His family and other children had lived seasonally in Akhadi; grandparents stayed through winter. Residents cited isolation, lack of a road and transport, and other hardships. The report describes the only access then as a footpath and notes that the village had no electricity. By 2014 the census recorded no permanent residents. A 2021 television feature later showed a young family member returning to farm there seasonally; it does not establish permanent residence. ([source](https://mtisambebi.ge/news/people/item/368-%E2%80%9Eaxadshi-dabrunebaz-gvinda-da-darchenaz%2C-magram-arz-gzaa-da-arz-kvali%E2%80%9C))

## Crafts And Traditions
- Reported livelihoods include cattle raising, potatoes and other vegetables, and dairy production. A 2021 feature documents traditional cattle breeding and production of Pshav dried cottage cheese and Gudi cheese from livestock taken to alpine pasture. The village’s Pirkushi shrine is associated regionally with blacksmithing; no dated Akhadi feast day was verified. ([source](https://1tv.ge/video/ferma-studenti-romelmac-ukana-fshavshi-dzveli-kera-gaacockhla/))
- A local-history report says residents observed Mikha Khelashvili’s memorial gathering at his former homestead and performed the traditional shilindi. The annual Mikhaoba commemoration is held in Chargali on 25 January, not established as a feast in Akhadi itself. ([source](https://kvirispalitra.ge/article/61127-qmikha-khelashvili-sagvareulo-sadzvaleshi-unda-daikrdzalosq/))

## Churches, Towers, Monuments
- A 2017 report identifies the Khelashvili family house site and the historic Goderdzauli fortress as local heritage residents wanted restored. It does not establish either one’s formal heritage-register status. No village church or tower with a verified register entry was located. ([source](https://mtisambebi.ge/news/people/item/368-%E2%80%9Eaxadshi-dabrunebaz-gvinda-da-darchenaz%2C-magram-arz-gzaa-da-arz-kvali%E2%80%9C))
