## History
- Chadistsikhe (also rendered Chadis Tsikhe) is an abandoned settlement in the Pasanauri community on the eastern slope of the Alevi Ridge. The village profile reports no inhabitants in either the 2002 or 2014 census. In November 2025, a regional report listed one household in Chadistsikhe under a government solar-power program, while warning that the list was inaccurate and included households that lived in some highland villages seasonally. This is not a verified headcount. I found no reliable source establishing its founding, first written mention, former resident families, reason or date of abandonment, or last inhabitants. ([source](https://explore-georgia.ge/en/mtskheta-mtianeti/dusheti-municipality/chadistsikhe))
