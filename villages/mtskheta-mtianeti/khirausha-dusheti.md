## History
- Khirausha lies on the Khorkhi River in the Zhinvali community and is now described as abandoned. A 2019 report says that in 1985 the Zhinvali Reservoir submerged the valleys former road, after which the long, difficult detour and school closures contributed to depopulation; it identifies Khirausha as one of two fully emptied villages in the Khorkhi valley. The report does not name Khiraushas last residents or give a precise date when its final household left. ([source](https://mtisambebi.ge/news/people/item/1097-gagma-napirze-ertmanetis-imedad-reportaji-xorxis-xeobidan))
- A 1923 ethnic-composition table lists 108 people in Khirausha, all recorded as Georgian. This is the earliest population record located here, not a founding date. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-ethnic-loc1923.htm))
