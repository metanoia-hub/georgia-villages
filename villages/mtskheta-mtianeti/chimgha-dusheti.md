## History
- Chimgha lies in the Arkhoti Gorge of Khevsureti, on the left bank of the Chimghistsqali River. The located population series is 74 in 1926, 5 in 2002, and 1 in 2014. Georgian Travel Guide reports that one person, Gigla Ochiuri, had lived there alone for years; the page gives no date for this report. A 2020 travel account instead describes Chimgha as having become depopulated in recent years. These sources do not establish a founding date or explain when and why the resident population fell to one or zero. Georgian Travel Guide reports that the 2014 census recorded all residents as Georgian. ([source](https://georgiantravelguide.com/ka/chimghas-tetraulebis-koshki))
- A 2023 report on a lawsuit seeking a safe road says Chimgha was among Khevsureti villages without road access, limiting access to emergency services. A separate travel account says the Arkhoti valley had already lost Chimghas population before a new road was opened toward Akhieli and Amgha in 2018. ([source](https://www.sakartvelosambebi.ge/en/news/court-accepts-lawsuit-for-establishing-safe-road-in-borderline-khevsureti))
