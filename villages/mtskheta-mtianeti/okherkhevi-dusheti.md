## History
- Okherkhevi is an abandoned village on the right side of the Khevsureti Aragvi. During the Soviet period it belonged to Barisakho rural council and stock raising was developed there. Sources reviewed do not establish the settlement’s founding or first record, why and when it emptied, or who its last inhabitants were. ([source](https://explore-georgia.ge/en/mtskheta-mtianeti/dusheti-municipality/okherkhevi))

## Population Over Time (Other Sources)
- 29 residents in 1917; 36 in 1923; and 0 in 2002, 2014, and 2024. Explore Georgia reports 44 residents in the 1926 census and none in 2002 or 2014. ([source](https://pop-stat.mashke.org/georgia-census2024.htm))
