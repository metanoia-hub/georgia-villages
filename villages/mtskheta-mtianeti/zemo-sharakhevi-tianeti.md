## History
- Zemo Sharakhevi belongs to the Zaridzeebi administrative unit. A Russian-language settlement entry places it on the left bank of the Sharakhevi River, about 17 km northwest of Tianeti, and says it had no permanent residents in the 2014 census. A dictionary entry says the upper and lower villages share the older collective name Sharakhevi. No village-specific founding date, documented reason or date of abandonment, or last residents were found. ([source](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%97%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%BE-%D0%A8%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%85%D0%B5%D0%B2%D0%B8))
- A 1954 Soviet decree transferred the Sharakhevi village council, including Zemo Sharakhevi and neighbouring settlements, to Zaridzeebi council. It documents administrative history, not the villages founding or depopulation. ([source](https://dspace.nplg.gov.ge/bitstream/1234/283641/2/Umaglesi_Sabchos_Uwyebebi_1954_N5_Rus.pdf))
- An 1876 travel account records the Sharakhevi settlement/community in the wider Tianeti area; it does not distinguish the present upper village with certainty. ([source](https://drevlit.ru/docs/kavkaz/XIX/1860-1880/Radde_G_I/text2.php))
