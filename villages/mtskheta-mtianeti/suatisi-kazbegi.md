## History
- Suatisi is in the Truso Gorge, on the right bank of the Suatisi River, at about 2,320 m. A historical study records 9 households and 64 people in 1802, then 28 households and 111 people in 1831–32, 29 households in 1860 and 1873, and 34 households in 1886. Before the 1944 deportation, the village had more than 50 households, including the Batsiev, Agkatsiev, Sambegov, Bekoev, Tkhostov, Bizikov, Kasaev and Eloev families. The village was emptied by the 1944 deportation; sources located do not name the final inhabitants individually.
