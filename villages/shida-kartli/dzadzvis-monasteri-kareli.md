## History
- Dzadzvis Monasteri is a deserted village in Kareli Municipality whose name refers to Dzadzvi Monastery. The sources found do not give a village founding date, earliest settlement record, or the dates and causes of depopulation. Russian-language municipal material lists it among settlements that became entirely depopulated, without naming last residents. ([source](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%9A%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D0%BC%D1%83%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%86%D0%B8%D0%BF%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5%D1%82))
- A monastery at Dzadzvi was consecrated in 2014 as a male monastery dedicated to the Nativity of John the Baptist. The source describes restoration and an ancient religious foundation but does not date the village itself. ([source](https://www.mematiane.ge/church.php?id=1965))

## Churches, Towers, Monuments
- Dzadzvi Monastery, dedicated to the Nativity of John the Baptist, is a functioning male monastery; the 2014 report records its consecration. An architectural video describes a stone church and carved iconostasis. Heritage-register status was not verified in the official registry search.
