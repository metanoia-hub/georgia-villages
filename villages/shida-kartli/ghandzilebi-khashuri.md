## History
- Ghandzilebi is a deserted highland settlement in Khashuri Municipality, historically within the domain of the Tsitsishvili. It does not appear in the historical works of Vakhushti or Ioane Batonishvili, nor in the historical or economic records consulted by the settlement profile. Families recorded there in 1945 included Barbakadze, Gasishvili, Gorelishvili, Papashvili, Nakhapetiani, Paksashvili, Lursmanashvili, and Bitsadze. Residents were resettled to Khashuri in the 1950s and the village broke up. ([source](https://explore-georgia.ge/en/shida-kartli/khashuri-municipality/ghandzilebi))
