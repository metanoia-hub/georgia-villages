## History
- Didi Khavleti is a former village in the Tsromi administrative unit. The national archives Tsromi entry identifies it as an abandoned settlement about 15 km from Khashuri. The sources located do not establish a founding date, earliest inhabitants, cause of depopulation, or named last inhabitants. A 2023 study describes it among Khashuri highland villages with seasonal residents, so the census absence should not be read as proof nobody ever visits or stays seasonally. ([source](https://erekle.archive.gov.ge/places/506/tsromi))
- A 2008 demographic study reports 35 residents in its earlier population table and says the villages Ossetian residents had migrated mainly to North Ossetia; it does not give a village-specific departure date. The studys characterization should be read alongside the later census counts and seasonal-resident research. ([source](https://www.files.ethz.ch/isn/106675/working_paper_45_en.pdf))
