## History
- Jvartmukha is an abandoned highland village in Tskhramukha administrative unit and belonged to the historical domain of the Tsitsishvili. It is not mentioned as a settlement by Vakhushti or Ioane Batonishvili and does not appear in the Georgian historical documents consulted. The 1886 census counted 16 households (136 people), wholly Georgian and said to have come mainly from Imereti. By 1925 there were 39 households, including Gogaladze, Lomidze, Chkheidze, Kurtanidze, Nozadze, Murjikneli, and Razmadze families. Residents were settled in Khashuri during the 1960s; the village was officially abolished in 1966. ([source](https://explore-georgia.ge/en/shida-kartli/khashuri-municipality/jvartmukha))

## Churches, Towers, Monuments
- Ruins of St Georges Church survive at Jvartmukha. The Church of St George at Nasaqdari, south of the village, was restored in 1986 on the ruins of an older church. Official heritage-register status for either site was not confirmed in the register searches conducted. ([source](https://explore-georgia.ge/en/shida-kartli/khashuri-municipality/jvartmukha))
