## History
- Ghvtismshobeli is in the Tori basin, on the right bank of the Gujaretistsqali. It formerly belonged to Gverdisubani community and is now in the Tsaghveri administrative unit. The available sources give no founding or first-record date, and no sourced account of why it emptied or who its last residents were. A 2016 Georgian-language feature identifies the village with its name and location but does not supply that missing chronology. ## Churches, Towers, Monuments
- Church of the Mother of God: a medieval hall church with south and west annexes, built of differently coloured dressed tufa. It has been a listed cultural heritage monument since 2006 and is reported badly damaged, with part of the apse and conch collapsed.
